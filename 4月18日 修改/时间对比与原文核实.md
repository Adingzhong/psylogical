## 三、各范式原文 verbatim 核实

### 3.1 Flanker — Krueger CE, et al. (2009). **Neurology** 73(5):349-355.

**期刊**: Neurology (AAN 官方期刊, 中科院医学大类 1 区, IF 9.9, Q1 临床神经科学)

**原文 verbatim**(WebFetch 提取自 PMC2725928 Methods 段):
> "The stimuli were presented until the subject pressed a key (with a maximum exposure of 4 seconds)."
>
> "The interstimulus interval was a random amount of time and varied between 1 and 3 seconds."

**解读**:
- **4 秒**是绝对上限(maximum exposure),**self-paced** 为主
- 适用人群:早期额颞痴呆(FTD)患者 + 健康对照 — 与我们的 MCI 目标群体高度相似

**建议**:`maxResponse: 2000 → 4000ms`,**有原文直接依据** ✅

---

### 3.2 SART — Robertson IH, et al. (1997). **Neuropsychologia** 35(6):747-758.

**期刊**: Neuropsychologia (现为心理学 3 区, 但 **Google Scholar 2879 引用** — SART 范式奠基作,权威无替代)

**原文 verbatim**(本地 pdftotext 提取并破解 PDF 数字位移编码后):

> "In the SART procedure, 225 single digits (25 of each of the nine digits) were presented visually over a 4.3-min period."
>
> "Each digit was presented for 250 msec, followed by a 900-msec mask."
>
> "The period from digit onset to digit onset was 1150 msec."
>
> "Subjects were asked to give equal importance to accuracy and speed in doing the task."

**PDF 编码破解说明**: Robertson 1997 PDF 用了古老的字符集,数字显示为"149 msec" / "899!msec" / "0049 msec"。每个数字 +1 后 mod 10 可还原:
- 149 → 250 ✅
- 899 → 900 ✅
- 0049 → 1150 ✅
- 114 trials → 225 ✅
- 14 targets → 25 ✅

**解读**:
- Robertson 原版 **SOA 1150ms**(即 250 刺激 + 900 mask)
- 没有独立响应窗 — 响应机会就是 1150ms 一整个 SOA
- 我们当前 **2500ms** 已经比原版宽松一倍多

**建议**: **保持 2500ms 不变**,有原文依据(Robertson 1997 原版 1150ms,我们已放宽,无需再改)

### 3.3 N-back — Suzuki M, et al. (2018). ***Frontiers in Aging Neuroscience*** 10:358.

**标题**: Neural Correlates of Working Memory Maintenance in Advanced Aging: Evidence From fMRI
**期刊**: 中科院医学大类 **2 区**, IF 4.1, Q2
**对象**: 高龄老年人(77-82 岁) + 年轻老年人(61-70 岁)

**原文 verbatim**(本地 pdftotext 从 PDF 提取):

> "Each item (the face or the dot) appeared for 2000 ms, with a stimulus onset asynchrony (SOA) of 4000 ms."
>
> "A black central fixation cross (+) was presented throughout the inter-item interval for 2000 ms."
>
> "Participants were instructed to respond as quickly as possible without sacrificing accuracy."
>
> "All responses were made with the index ('yes') and middle ('no') finger of the right hand using an MRI-compatible keypad."

**解读**:
- 刺激呈现 **2000ms**
- ISI **2000ms**
- **SOA 4000ms** = 有效响应窗
- 任务:face 0-back / 1-back 和 location 0-back / 1-back
- **注意**:这是面孔/位置 N-back,**不是图片物体 N-back**。但任务复杂度相似

**建议**:
- N-back B1 / B2: `resp_deadline: 3000 → 4000ms`,有原文依据 ✅
- N-back B3 (图片 + 3 选项):因为需要点击选项,保留 **5000ms** — 这 1000ms 的额外余量是为选择按钮的运动时间。**无直接原文依据**,是推理

### 3.4 VSTMB — Parra MA, et al. (2025). *Aging, Neuropsychology, and Cognition*

**引用**: Parra et al. 2025 "Oculomotor behaviors during visual short-term memory binding in healthy aging" (JCR Q2)
**核心说明**:这篇 2025 论文明确注明 *"The change detection paradigm was previously described by Parra et al. (2009)"* — 即沿用 Parra 2009 原始范式

**原文 verbatim**(本地 pdftotext 提取自 strathprints 开放版 PDF):

> "Trials began with a fixation screen (a cross) shown for 250 ms, followed by a study display for 2000 ms, an unfilled retention interval of 900 ms and finally the test display, presented for 4000 ms (Figure 1)."
>
> "In half of the trials, objects on both displays were the same, whereas, in the other half, objects displayed different colors in the test display."
>
> "Participants were requested to detect whether the study and test displays consisted of the 'same' or 'different' objects by selecting either 'S' or 'D' on the keyboard in front of them."

**解读**:
- 编码 **2000ms** ✅ 与当前代码匹配
- 维持 **900ms** ✅ 与当前代码匹配
- 测试显示 **4000ms** — 当前代码是 5000ms,**反而比原文宽松**
- **样本**是健康老年人(不是 MCI),时长未特别延长

**建议**:
- VSTMB timeout: **维持 5000ms 不变** 或 **改成 4000ms 对齐原文**
- **不要改成 7000ms** — 原文没有支持,我之前是拍脑袋
- 保守建议:**维持 5000ms**,比原文宽松 1 秒对适老更友好
